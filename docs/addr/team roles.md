## Team Communication

### Daily Standup (15 min)
- **Who**: All engineering roles
- **When**: 9:30 AM daily
- **Format**: What did you do? What will you do? Any blockers?

### Sprint Planning (2 hours)
- **Who**: All team members
- **When**: First day of sprint (bi-weekly)
- **Format**: Review backlog, estimate stories, commit to sprint

### Sprint Review (1 hour)
- **Who**: All team + stakeholders
- **When**: Last day of sprint
- **Format**: Demo completed features, gather feedback

### Sprint Retrospective (1 hour)
- **Who**: Engineering team
- **When**: After sprint review
- **Format**: What went well? What didn't? Action items

### Technical Sync (1 hour)
- **Who**: Technical Lead + Team Leads
- **When**: Weekly (Wednesday)
- **Format**: Technical challenges, architecture decisions

### Product Review (30 min)
- **Who**: Product Manager + Technical Lead
- **When**: Weekly (Monday)
- **Format**: Roadmap review, priority adjustments
